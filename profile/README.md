# Archive of Deleted Repositories

Archival copies of public GitHub repositories deleted by their authors.

## Policy

- Repositories here are archival copies of projects deleted by their authors from GitHub.
- Secrets found in the history (API keys, tokens, private keys) are replaced with `***REMOVED***` before publication. Commit hashes therefore differ from the originals.
- Repositories taken down for legal reasons are not published.
- Copies are read-only and not maintained.
